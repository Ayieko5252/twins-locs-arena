How booking works

Choose your service

Pick from the loc menu. Tell us roughly how many locs you have and how long they are, so we set aside enough time.

Pick a time

We have two chairs. Greyed-out times are fully booked, so pick any time that's still open.

Pay the deposit

We'll confirm by phone, and a 30% deposit holds your slot. You pay the rest after your session.

Loc care between visits

Book a retwist every 4–6 weeks and a detox every 3 months. Sleep in a satin bonnet or loc sock, and spritz your locs lightly instead of soaking them.
