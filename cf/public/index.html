Booking

How booking works

  1. Choose your service

    Pick from the loc menu. Tell us roughly how many locs you have and how long they are, so we set aside enough time.

  2. Pick a time

    We have two chairs. Greyed-out times are fully booked, so pick any time that is still open.

  3. Pay the deposit

    We confirm by phone, and a 30% deposit holds your slot. You pay the rest after your session.
