Put the salon on your home screen

The app is the same Twins Locs Arena you use in the browser: book a chair, shop loc care, see your appointments and offers. It opens full screen, with no address bar, and it keeps working when the signal drops.

Android steps iPhone steps

The app is already installed on this phone. Open it from your home screen.

Android

  1. Open www.twinslocsarena.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app if you see it.
  4. Tap Install. The loc icon appears with your other apps.

Samsung Internet: tap the menu, then Add page to, then Home screen.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open www.twinslocsarena.com in Safari. It must be Safari, not Chrome.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom, the square with an arrow.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right.

Works on iOS 16.4 and newer.
